In the afternoon, after we had made love, I lay looking at the light outside the window, and felt nothing but a vague, immeasurable bewilderment.

For some reason, the verses of *Qingping Diao* rose in my mind. Love, lover, to love. My motherland. What an awkward word it was, dragged so stiffly from English into Mandarin, as though a place might be made one's own simply by giving it a name.

Everything seemed an illusion. Our lives, perhaps, were nothing more than passing forms, assuming shapes which we mistook for reality.

And perhaps we had been living in the cyber age all along. We met on dating applications, talked, and shared with one another the small, rather miserable particulars of our lives, and all those little entertainments by which we kept ourselves pleasantly sedated. We held hands. We kissed. We made love. We came. And then, once more, and once more again.

But perhaps the only thing that truly mattered was to acknowledge that 1+1=2.

For once one had learnt that 1+1=2, there could be no existential crisis, no terrible uncertainty of being. In the midst of all these fraudulent grand narratives, one must abandon logic, abandon the demand for coherence, and kneel before that single eternal truth.

1+1=2.

And only by believing it could one live.

Go on living.
